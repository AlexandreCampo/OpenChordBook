# Privacy

OpenChordBook — Copyright © 2026 Alexandre Campo

Your charts, folders and preferences stay on your device. There are no accounts,
ads, analytics or trackers, and the app does not upload your library.
Imports are read locally; exports are saved wherever you choose.

Tunes are not packaged with the app. They are downloaded by the user.
A download contacts the source you select. The web version also contacts its
hosting site to load the app; fonts and libraries are included locally.

Export your tunes to keep a copy or transfer them between devices. Uninstalling
the app or clearing its storage removes the local library.
