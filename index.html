    Sections, meter changes & repeats
    /

    Select whole bars in the chord field, then repeat them. With no selection, repeats the current bar.

    Chord notation

    Use spaces for chords in the same bar: Dm7 G7 | Cmaj7. Up to four chords fit in a bar; two chords split it evenly.

    Minor: Dm7 or D-7
    Major: Cmaj7 or CΔ7
    Altered: G7b9, G7#5, G7alt
    Other: F#dim7, Bø7, Csus4, C6/9
    Slash chord: Cmaj7/E

    % repeats the previous bar. Use |: Dm7 | G7 :| for repeat barlines. Put [AABA] or [Bridge] before a section. Use [3/4] at a bar to change meter until the next change.
